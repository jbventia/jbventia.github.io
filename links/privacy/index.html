JCVentia / Site information

Privacy, simply explained.

JCVentia is the independent Amway business of Jaron and Codie. This site helps you explore products and contact us.

Shopping and payments

Product purchases, payment details, shipping information and order accounts are handled on Amway's website. Review Amway's privacy notice when shopping.

Find your fit and contacting us

Find your fit keeps your choices in the current page while you use the tool. If you choose to share a summary by email, your email app prepares the message; you decide whether to send it. Email, text, call and Instagram links open those services. Information you send to Jaron and Codie is used to respond to your questions and provide the assistance you request.

Third-party services

Amway's wellness and beauty tools, Instagram, Shop Ahead and Avellum have their own terms and privacy practices. Avellum health information and medical intake are handled through its service and clinical partners, not on this website. JCVentia does not provide medical care.

Website hosting

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information needed to operate and secure the website. See GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.
